Calabar Fringe welcomes submissions from artists anywhere in the world. Every entry is read by a selection panel.

Short film Films up to 20 minutes, completed on or after 1 January 2024, any genre. Entries are taken through FilmFreeway, and Nigerian filmmakers enter free. Final deadline 6 November 2026, with selections announced by 20 November.
Theatre New plays up to one hour, and shorter pieces for the daily programme.
Music, comedy, poetry, dance Live acts for the daily programme.
Visual art Work for the exhibition that runs through the festival days.
